git, with a retro soul.

DevTree is a GitKraken-style git client in a single ~2.5 MB Rust binary. Open it in your browser, import a repository, and get a commit graph, a conflict editor, line-by-line staging and interactive rebase. No Electron, no account, no telemetry.

Up in a minute

You need Rust and git. Build it, run it, and DevTree opens in your browser at http://localhost:7878.

Full install guide →

$ git clone https://github.com/ErmanDev/DevTree.git
$ cd DevTree
$ cargo run --release
🌳 DevTree running at http://localhost:7878

Everything you reach for every day

⑂

Commit graph

Colored lanes with curved merges, author pictures in the nodes, branch and tag badges. It stays smooth with tens of thousands of commits.

±

Stage lines, not files

Click the lines you want and stage, unstage or discard just those. A diff viewer with side-by-side view, syntax colors and word-level changes.

⚠

Conflict editor

Each conflict side by side: keep yours, take theirs, keep both, or edit by hand. Then Continue, Skip or Abort from one banner.

⣿

Interactive rebase

Drag commits to reorder, then pick, reword, squash, fixup or drop. New messages survive conflict pauses.

↓↑

Pull & push

Live progress with Cancel, auto-fetch, and a sign-in prompt for HTTPS remotes. Your SSH keys just work.

⌕

History & blame

File history that follows renames, blame colored by age, search across all history, and compare any two commits or branches.

⊞

Branches, tags, stash

Drag a branch onto another to merge or rebase. Cherry-pick, revert and reset from the menu. Stash with one click.

☺

Author chips

See who wrote and who committed each change, with pictures from GitHub or Gravatar. Or initials only, if you prefer.

◐

Retro, light or dark

Claude-warm colors, pixel headings and faint scanlines, in a dark or a cream light theme.

Merge conflicts, defused

When a merge, rebase or cherry-pick stops, a banner tells you exactly where you are. Open the editor to resolve each conflict:

  • Use current, incoming, or both, or edit by hand
  • Keep a whole file from one side in one click
  • Continue, skip or abort without the command line

Commit exactly what you meant

Select individual lines, or whole hunks, and stage them. Unstage or discard a single line just as easily. Word-level highlights show what actually changed within a line.

Rewrite history calmly

"Interactive rebase from here…" lists the commits on your branch. Drag them into order, choose an action for each, and check the summary before you start.

Know who changed what

Blame shows the author of every line, brighter for newer changes. Click a line to see its commit. File history follows renames.
